Our Story

A Warm Home in the Mountains

Nestled just 50-55 km from Kutaisi, close to the E60 highway, Ubisa Family Guesthouse is your perfect sanctuary. Whether you're a road tripper seeking an authentic stop or a traveler seeking mountain tranquility, we welcome you with open arms.

Experience true Georgian hospitality. Our family prepares delicious traditional dishes, from our famous Khachapuri to homemade wine. Every meal tells a story of generations.

What Awaits You

Unforgettable Experiences

Everything you need for a rejuvenating stay immersed in nature and culture.

Authentic Cuisine

Wake up to hearty breakfasts with fresh pastries. Savor our signature Khachapuri and traditional Georgian dishes prepared with love.

Homemade Wine

Taste wine made with family tradition and pride. Experience Georgian winemaking heritage in every sip, shared with stories and warmth.

Nature & Mountains

Balconies with breathtaking mountain views. Explore gardens, hike nearby trails, fish in pristine waters, and breathe in pure mountain air.

BBQ & Gatherings

Perfect your grilling skills with our barbecue facilities. Create memories with friends and family under the Georgian sky.
