Our Story

A Home Away From Home in the Mountains

Situated just 50-55 km from Kutaisi and close to the E60 highway, Ubisa Family Guesthouse is your perfect stopover on a Georgian road trip or a dedicated escape from city life.

We offer a unique opportunity to experience authentic local life. My family prepares delicious dishes from Georgian cuisine, including our famous Khachapuri and homemade wine. Whether you want to hike, fish, or simply relax on the terrace, our doors are open.

Getting Here

Well Connected in Imereti

Ubisa Family Guesthouse sits right by the E60, roughly 50–55 km from Kutaisi and about 200 km from Tbilisi — an easy stop whether you're passing through or staying a while.

By Car

From Tbilisi, follow the E60 west; the drive takes around 2.5–3 hours. From Kutaisi, it's about an hour. Free parking is available right at the house.

By Public Transport

Marshrutkas heading from Tbilisi toward Kutaisi pass through Ubisa — just ask the driver to stop there. Let us know your arrival time and we're happy to help you find the way from the main road.
